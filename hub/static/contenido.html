Content review (cards)

Everything a member sees or hears comes from these cards (content/cards.json). Nothing is made up on the fly. Each language stays UNVERIFIED until a person reviews it: an agronomist or the extension officer for the advice, a native speaker for each language's text and voice. English is the main language. The Kiswahili and Gĩkũyũ texts are AI drafts and their audio is provisional synthetic speech (an English voice reading them); native speakers must review the texts and record the audio here.

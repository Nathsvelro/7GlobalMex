## (f) Why we split by group: leakage check

Same shipped model on 1800 unused copies (flips/rotations/colour shifts/burst shots) of TRAINING photos, i.e. what a naive random split would have put in the test set. Compare with the group-split test: accuracy **99.5%**, macro-F1 **0.995** vs. **98.5%** / **0.985** on the group-split test.
